Pricing FAQ

These rules help keep account, refund, and abuse decisions clear for commercial review.

Can I request a refund?

Unused paid balance may be eligible for manual review. Used credits and consumed API usage are generally not refundable.

What happens to unused balance?

Unused paid balance remains available for eligible API consumption unless your account is closed, suspended, refunded, or otherwise restricted under the Terms.

Are promotional credits refundable?

No. Promotional, bonus, trial, or test credits are usually not refundable and may expire or be removed according to campaign terms.

What about abuse accounts?

Accounts involved in fraud, abuse, policy violations, chargebacks, or security risk may be suspended and may be ineligible for refunds.

Is every model always available?

No. Model availability depends on account settings, enabled channels, upstream availability, compliance checks, and current dashboard configuration.

Are rate limits fixed?

Rate limits may vary by plan, account history, payment status, model, routing channel, abuse controls, and operational constraints.
