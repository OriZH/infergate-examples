Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how InferGate handles information for its website, dashboard, API console, support operations, and independent AI API gateway service.

Information we collect

We may collect account identifiers, email address, authentication records, billing and payment references, API key metadata, API usage records, balance activity, service logs, device and security signals, and support communications.

How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, deliver API gateway services, process payments, show usage and balance records, provide support, prevent abuse, investigate fraud, comply with legal obligations, and improve service reliability.

API content and logs

InferGate may process request metadata, routing information, error logs, security signals, and limited request or response data when required to provide the service, troubleshoot issues, enforce policies, or protect the platform. Customers should avoid sending sensitive personal data unless they have a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

Service providers

We may use third-party processors for cloud hosting, payment processing, analytics, email, support, monitoring, security, and fraud prevention. These providers process information as needed to operate and protect InferGate.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with service providers, payment partners, security vendors, legal advisers, authorities when required by law, or parties involved in a business transfer.

Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, enforce terms, maintain security, meet accounting requirements, and comply with applicable law.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and operational safeguards designed to protect accounts, credentials, usage records, and service systems. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your requests

For privacy, account, or data requests, contact support@useinfergate.com. We may verify your identity before taking action.
