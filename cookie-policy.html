Cookie Policy

How InferGate uses essential, security, Turnstile, session, and trusted-device cookies.

Policy version2026.07.27Last updated2026-07-27

This page explains the current policy in plain language. It is not legal advice for a specific situation and remains subject to professional review under applicable law.

InferGate uses a limited set of essential and security cookies. We do not use cookies to sell personal data.

Essential cookies

Authentication and session cookies keep you signed in and protect requests. Security cookies support CSRF protection, Cloudflare Turnstile, rate limiting, and abuse prevention.

Trusted-device cookie

When a Root user chooses to trust a verified device, a secure HttpOnly credential may reduce repeated MFA prompts for up to 30 days. The server stores only a hash of the random device credential. Password changes, MFA reset, risk actions, and sign-out from all devices invalidate trust.

Your choices

Blocking or clearing essential cookies can sign you out, require MFA again, or prevent secure account actions. Browser settings can remove stored cookies at any time.
