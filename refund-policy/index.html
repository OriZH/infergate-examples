Refund Policy

This policy applies to InferGate digital API credits, paid balance, and usage-based API gateway services.

Digital API credits

InferGate provides a usage-based API service. Paid balance and top-ups are used for API consumption, including model requests routed through the InferGate gateway.

Unused paid balance

Unused paid balance may be eligible for a manual refund review. Refund eligibility depends on account status, payment records, usage history, fraud checks, and compliance with the Terms of Service.

Usually not refundable

Used credits, consumed API usage, promotional credits, bonus credits, test credits, accounts involved in abuse, and accounts that violate the Terms of Service are usually not refundable.

How to request a refund

Contact support@useinfergate.com with your account email, payment reference if available, requested refund amount, and a short reason for the request.

Processing time

After a refund is approved, processing usually takes 5-10 business days. Actual timing may depend on the payment provider, card issuer, bank, or other payment rail involved.
