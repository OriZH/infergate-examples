Build a fair comparison

  • Select representative tasks and define pass criteria before seeing outputs.
  • Keep prompts and output limits comparable across models.
  • Record actual returned identity, complete status and non-empty text.
  • Measure latency and billed usage across multiple requests, not a single best result.
  • Inspect failure modes separately from successful outputs.
